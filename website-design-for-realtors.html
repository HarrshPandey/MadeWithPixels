FAQs

COMMON
QUESTIONS.

Can I update property listings myself?
Yes. We build with a CMS letting you add, edit, and remove listings without a developer.
Do you integrate with MagicBricks or 99acres?
We build standalone sites. The goal is to own the lead — not pay a portal commission.
How do property buyers contact me?
Via WhatsApp, contact forms, callback buttons, and site visit booking forms. All leads go directly to your phone or email.
Can you build a website for a new residential project?
Yes. Project microsites with unit plans, price range, amenities, RERA number, and booking enquiry.

Location-specific pages, Google Business Profile optimisation, and property schema markup — all built into your site.
