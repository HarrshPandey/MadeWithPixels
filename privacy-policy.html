01

About This Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how MadeOfPixels Studio (“we”, “us”, “the Studio”) collects, uses, stores, shares and protects personal information when you visit madeofpixels.in, contact us by form, email, phone or WhatsApp, or become a client. We are based in Vasai West, Maharashtra, India, and we decide why and how the personal information described here is processed.

By using our website or sending us your information, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the website or submit personal information to us.

03

How We Use Your Information

We use personal information to:

  • Reply to your enquiries and prepare quotes and proposals.
  • Plan, deliver, support and invoice the services you ask for.
  • Keep in touch with you about your project, including updates, feedback and changes.
  • Keep the website secure, prevent misuse and fix technical problems.
  • Analyse how the website is used so that we can improve it and our services.
  • Meet legal, tax and accounting obligations, and establish or defend legal claims.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use it to make automated decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you. We contact you only about your enquiry or project unless you have agreed to hear from us about something else.

04

Consent and Legal Basis

We process your personal information with your consent, for example when you submit a form or message us. We also process it where necessary to carry out a contract with you, to comply with the law, or for other uses that applicable law permits.

You can withdraw your consent at any time by emailing hello@madeofpixels.in. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that took place before you withdrew it, and it may mean we can no longer respond to your enquiry or provide a service that depends on that information.

05

Cookies, Analytics and Similar Technologies

We use a small number of tools that set cookies or use similar technologies, such as identifiers and browser storage.

  • Google Analytics reports on aggregated traffic and usage of the site.
  • Microsoft Clarity provides behaviour analytics such as heatmaps and session replays that show where visitors click and scroll.
  • Google Fonts delivers our typefaces. Your browser requests them from Google’s servers, so Google receives your IP address.
  • Local storage remembers your light or dark display preference. It does not identify you.

We do not use advertising or remarketing cookies on this website. You can control or delete cookies in your browser settings, or use Google’s Analytics opt-out browser add-on. Blocking these tools will not stop you using the site. Because they are run by their providers, the providers’ own privacy policies also apply.

07

International Transfers

Some of our providers store or process data outside India, including in the United States. By using the website and sending us your information, you understand that it may be transferred to and processed in those countries. We choose established providers, rely on their security measures and commitments, and transfer personal data only as applicable Indian law permits.

08

How Long We Keep Information

  • Enquiries that do not lead to a project are kept for as long as needed to respond and follow up, and are then deleted.
  • Client records are kept for the duration of the engagement and afterwards for as long as tax, accounting and other laws require, or as needed to deal with a dispute.
  • Analytics data is kept according to the retention settings of the analytics providers.
  • Deletion. When information is no longer needed, we delete or anonymise it securely. If you ask us to delete your personal data, we will do so unless the law requires us to keep it.
09

How We Protect Information

We use reasonable security practices to protect personal information, including access controls, encrypted HTTPS connections, protected accounts and careful choice of service providers. These are described in our Information Security Policy.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. If a personal data breach affects you, we will notify you and the authorities as the law requires.

10

Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 as its provisions come into force, you may:

  • Access a summary of the personal data we process about you and the providers we have shared it with.
  • Correct, complete or update personal data that is inaccurate or out of date.
  • Erase your personal data when it is no longer needed for the purpose it was collected for.
  • Withdraw your consent at any time.
  • Raise a grievance about how your data is handled.
  • Nominate another person to exercise your rights if you die or become unable to do so.

To exercise a right, email hello@madeofpixels.in with the subject “Privacy Request”. We may ask you to verify your identity first, and we aim to respond within 30 days.

11

Children

Our website and services are meant for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has given us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

12
