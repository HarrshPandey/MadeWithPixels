FAQs

COMMON
QUESTIONS.

Do I need a website if I'm already on Zomato?
Yes — because you don't own Zomato. Your website is an asset you control that ranks on Google separately.
Can customers order food directly from my website?
Yes. Online ordering pages where customers place orders via WhatsApp — no commission to Zomato or Swiggy.
How do I display my menu?
As a digital menu — categories, items, descriptions, and prices — beautifully laid out and mobile-optimised.
Can the menu be updated easily?
Yes. We build with a simple CMS so you can update prices or add items from your phone.
Do you help restaurants rank on Google?
Yes. We optimise for 'restaurant in [area]' and 'best [cuisine] near me in [area]'.
How much does a restaurant website cost?
Basic sites from ₹15,000–₹25,000. With online ordering and booking from ₹30,000–₹60,000.
