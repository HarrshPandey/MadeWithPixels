FAQs

COMMON
QUESTIONS.

What pages does a school website need?
Home, About/Vision, Academics, Faculty, Facilities, Admissions, Events/News, Gallery, and Contact.
Can parents access notices and circulars online?
Yes. A digital notice board where school admins post circulars and announcements that parents access from the website.
Can the school update the website themselves?
Yes. We integrate a simple CMS so staff can update news, events, galleries, and circulars without technical knowledge.
How do we handle online admission enquiries?
A structured admissions form — students fill in details and upload documents. All submissions go to the admissions coordinator's email and WhatsApp.
Do you build for coaching classes too?
Yes. Coaching centres, tutorial classes, skill institutes, and vocational training centres — all served.
How much does a school website cost?
Basic information sites from ₹20,000–₹35,000. With CMS, admissions system, and parent portal from ₹40,000–₹80,000.
