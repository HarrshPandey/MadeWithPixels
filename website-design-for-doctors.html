FAQs

COMMON
QUESTIONS.

What pages does a doctor's website need?
At minimum: Home, About the Doctor, Services, Appointment Booking, Contact, and Location.
Can you add online appointment booking?
Yes. We integrate appointment booking forms, WhatsApp scheduling, and can connect to Calendly or Practo.
How do patients find my clinic on Google?
Through local SEO — optimising for '[specialty] doctor in [area]'. We build this into every medical site.
Is patient data secure?
Yes. We don't store sensitive patient data — enquiry forms send directly to your email or WhatsApp.
Can I have a bilingual website?
Yes. Many medical clients serve patients who prefer Hindi or Marathi. We build bilingual sites with a language toggle.
How much does a doctor's website cost?
Basic profile site from ₹12,000–₹20,000. Full clinic website with booking from ₹25,000–₹60,000.
